(04) — Process

From idea to live, in four steps.

  1. 01

    Discover

    A quick call to understand your business, goals and audience. You get a clear scope, timeline and quote — no jargon.

    You get Scope & plan

  2. 02

    Design

    Wireframes, then high-fidelity Figma designs for desktop and mobile. You review, I refine — until it feels unmistakably yours.

    You get Approved Figma design

  3. 03

    Build

    Clean, fast, accessible code. Responsive on every screen, SEO-ready, and animated where it makes the brand memorable.

    You get A staging link to test

  4. 04

    Launch

    Domain, hosting, analytics and a clean handover — so the site is yours to run, and easy to grow.

    You get A live website
